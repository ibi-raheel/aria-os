# ICP - Ideal Customer Profile

The single source of truth for **who we're targeting**. Stages 01, 02, and
03 all read this file. Edit here, not in lead files.

---

## The customer

**Creator earning $20k+/month** from creator-economy work, running a
community on **any platform** (Skool, Discord, Circle, Patreon, Teachable,
Kajabi, Mighty Networks, Maven, Slack, Telegram, or custom), who would
benefit from a unified spatial home for their community.

---

## Hard requirements (must have all)

| # | Requirement | How to verify |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Estimated MRR ≥ $20k USD | Member count × tier price; public revenue claims; course launch dollar mentions; visible pricing × audience size |
| 2 | Active on any social/community platform | Public profile, posting in last 30 days |
| 3 | Existing community (paying members or paid Discord/Skool) | Public community page, member count visible |
| 4 | Currently uses ≥2 of: Discord, Slack, Circle, Skool, Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi, Substack, Notion | Footer credits, link-in-bio, public community URL |
| 5 | Reachable on ≥2 channels (e.g., Twitter DM open + public email) | Profile inspection |
| 6 | Posts in own community at least weekly | Public community feed |

## Strong signals (boosts score)

- Recent complaints about current stack (Discord noise, lost messages, no structure)
- Recently launched / scaling / hiring
- Audience demographic skews 18-45 and is comfortable with gaming/interactive UIs
- Community size in the 100–5,000 paid-member sweet spot (too small = doesn't need it; too large = MVP can't serve)
- Has used or experimented with interactive/spatial tools before (gather.town, Discord activities, Whop)
- Visible team scale (manages community through assistants/mods) - has budget for DFY

## Disqualifiers (auto-reject)

- Already on Whop, Heartbeat, Geneva, or another spatial/community-OS tool
- Public anti-metaverse / anti-spatial / anti-gaming-UI stance
- Pure solo educator with no member base (no community to migrate)
- Audience is B2B-only enterprise (Arcadia is consumer/prosumer)
- Inactive for 60+ days (looks abandoned)
- Located in a region we can't realistically support (TBD - currently no constraint, revisit at scale)

## Niches that fit

- Online education / coaching / cohort-based courses
- Content creator with a paid community tier (Patreon, Substack paid, paid Discord)
- Skool / Circle community owners
- Creator-economy advisors or community-first founders

## Niches that don't fit (yet)

- B2B SaaS communities (different audience expectations)
- Hyper-technical dev communities (overlaps with Discord-native culture)
- One-time-purchase course creators with no community layer
- Pure newsletter writers without a community product

## Segmentation for first 10

Aim for variety so the case studies cover the spectrum:

- 3–4 Skool community owners (direct competitor migration story)
- 2–3 Discord/Circle community owners (community migration story)
- 2 cohort-based course operators (Maven/Teachable/Kajabi)
- 1–2 Patreon/Mighty Networks creators (platform consolidation story)
- 1–2 wildcard fits (a creator type we want to learn from)

## Volume model

- **Discovery:** 50 candidates/day from diverse sources (not just Skool)
- **Qualification:** light score gate, accept broadly
- **Enrichment:** deep dossier for all qualified leads (compute is free)
- **Personalization:** email + demo Loom for all; custom DMs for top 10
- **Send:** 50 emails/day (automated) + 50 templated DMs + 10 personalized DMs

## Refinement protocol

Update this file after every 5 outreaches. If reply patterns reveal a
sub-segment converts much better, narrow the ICP. Log each refinement as a
`memory/decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-icp-refinement-<slug>.md` entry.
