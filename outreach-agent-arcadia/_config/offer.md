# Offer

to talk about it**. Stage 04 (personalization) reads this. Edit here, not

## What it is

**Done-for-you Realm setup on Arcadia** - a hands-on engagement where we
migrate the creator's existing community into a custom Arcadia Realm,
configured around their brand, content, and members.

Includes:

- Realm theming and configuration to match their brand
- Course migration from existing platform (Teachable / Thinkific / Kajabi
  / Skool) into Arcadia Academy
- Member onboarding flow setup
- Community migration plan (Discord → Tavern, etc.) with member
  communication templates
- Live launch support
- 30 days of post-launch optimization

## What it costs

**Tentative range: $7,500 – $15,000** per engagement.

Notes on pricing:
- Don't quote a number in cold messages. Hold for the Calendly call.
- If pressed in DMs, frame as: *"Engagements typically run between $7.5k
  and $15k depending on community size and content volume - happy to walk
  through specifics on a quick call."*
- Refine after first 2-3 sales calls reveal what people will actually pay.
- Above $15k: needs case-study credibility we don't have yet.
- Below $7.5k: kills perceived authority; sounds like a freelancer side gig.

## Why this offer exists right now

Arcadia is freshly shipped (April 2026). Before going self-serve, we want
**10 paying creators** who:
- Validate the product in real production use
- Become the case studies for the platform launch
- Pay for our time so we can maintain runway during the validation phase

After 10 closed-won, the offer transitions to platform self-serve. The DFY
service may continue as a high-tier option but is not the primary motion.

## What's NOT included

Do not pitch:
- Custom Arcadia engineering (no source-code changes per customer)
- Multi-Realm support (MVP is single-Realm; multi is V2)
- Stripe / payment integration (out of MVP scope)
- Member-management tools (ban/mute/invite - not built)
- Mobile app (desktop-only MVP)
- Self-serve dashboard (MVP requires our setup help - that's the whole point)

## The Loom (the conversion asset)

**Hybrid format** - every Loom is recorded fresh per lead, but only the
30-second intro is personalized; the 2-minute core demo is reusable.

Structure:

1. **0:00–0:30** - Personalized intro using the Loom hooks from
   `memory/people/<name>.md` Profile section. ("Hey [name], saw your [recent
   thing], wanted to show you what your community would look like in
   Arcadia.")
2. **0:30–2:30** - Reusable core demo recorded once. Walks through avatar
   spawn → Tavern chat → Academy course view → Market browse → XP/level
   moment.
3. **2:30–3:00** - Personalized close. ("If this looks like your
   community's vibe, reply and I'll send a Calendly to talk it through.")

The Loom URL goes into every channel message as the central asset.

## CTA mechanic (locked)

**Watch the Loom → reply on any channel → user (Ibi) sends Calendly
manually.** No direct Calendly links in cold messages. Reply is the gate.
