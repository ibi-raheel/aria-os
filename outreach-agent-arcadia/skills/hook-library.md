# Hook Library

Opening hooks for cold messages. Keyed by trigger (what we noticed
about the lead) and by channel. These are **shape guides, not
fill-in-the-blank forms** - the actual message must be LLM-written
from the dossier.

**All hooks must comply with `_config/voice.md`.** Specific observation
+ genuine question. No filler openers, no stock closers. No links in
DM hooks.

---

## Hook structure

Every hook has three parts:

1. **The notice** - specific thing we observed (must be real, from the
   dossier)
2. **The connect** - bridge from their thing to our thing
3. **The ask** - single, soft, interest-based

**The structure should be invisible.** If a reader could outline
notice-connect-ask, rewrite it. These are prompts for the writer, not a
format for the reader.

---

## Rhythm rules (from voice.md - apply to every hook)

- No 3+ consecutive sentences of similar length
- At least one dash, parenthetical, or aside per message
- Conditional language is a strength ("not sure if," "might be worth")
- One imperfection per email is a feature - a P.S., a casual aside, a
  slightly informal transition
- If it sounds like a webinar script, rewrite it

## Subject lines for cold email

1-4 words. Lowercase. No punctuation. No brand name. No recipient name.
**Specific to the person's situation.** Mirror the exact topic, pivot,
or tension referenced in the message body.

Examples:
- `courses to community` (Creator E's pivot)
- `free charts vs. paid community` (Creator D's model)
- `Skool at 1,900 members` (Creator C's scale)
- `membership retention` (Stu McLaren's expertise)
- `the Milly Club structure` (Amy's multi-tier setup)

**Never:** "quick question," "community platform," "spatial communities,"
"thoughts?" - these are generic, not specific. They say nothing about
the person and compete with every other cold email in the inbox.

---

## CTAs (the ask)

Interest-based only on first touch. One per message. Never stack.

- "worth exploring?"
- "relevant to where you're focused?"
- "curious?"
- "might be off-base, but curious what you think."
- "want me to send it?" (DMs only - teasing the Loom)
- "want to see it?" (DMs only)

**Never on first touch:**
- Calendly links
- "let me know your thoughts"
- "I'd love to get on your calendar"
- "would love to chat AND get your feedback"
- Any double-ask

---

## What to never write

See `_config/voice.md` § "What we never say" and "Anti-AI tells" for the
complete list. Quick reference:

- Starting with "I"
- "Hope you're well" / "I was impressed by"
- "Quick question" / "I'll be brief..." / any filler opener
- "I'm building in X and trying not to solve a fake problem" / any stock closer
- "leverage" / "optimize" / "streamline" / any SAT word
- Feature lists or price mentions
- 3+ sentences of the same length in a row
- Anything that sounds like landing-page copy
