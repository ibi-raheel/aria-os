# Loom Script Template

Two types of Loom: the **reusable demo** (all leads) and the
**personalized intro** (top-10 leads). Stage 04 generates personalized
intro scripts. Ibi records both.

---

## Type 1: Reusable Demo (record once, well)

**Total length: 2:00-2:30 hard cap.**

Problem-first, not product-first. Every feature is introduced as a
pain-killer, not a capability.

### Second-by-second breakdown

```
0:00-0:05   FACE ON (cam-only, no screen share yet)
            "here's what your community looks like when it's
            not fragmented across five tabs."
            [Hook. Problem-first. No self-intro. No company name.
            Pure outcome statement.]

0:05-0:10   TRANSITION TO SCREEN+CAM
            Switch to screen share with face bubble bottom-left.
            Show the Arcadia world loading - the avatar spawn.
            "this is arcadia. your members show up as avatars
            in a world you own."

0:10-0:30   THE WORLD (the visual wow - front-load it)
            Walk the avatar through the space. Show the 2.5D
            environment. Let the visual breathe - minimal narration.
            "instead of a feed they scroll past, they walk
            through your space."
            [3-5 seconds of visual silence here. Let them absorb.]

0:30-0:55   THE TAVERN (community hub)
            Walk to Tavern. Show chat appearing. Another member
            visible.
            "this is where your members actually hang out.
            real-time, but they're together in a place - not a
            channel list."

0:55-1:20   THE ACADEMY (course delivery)
            Walk to Academy. Open a course. Show video playing.
            "your courses, workshops, cohort content - all inside
            the same world. no separate login. no teachable tab."

1:20-1:40   THE MARKET (monetization)
            Walk to Market. Browse a listing.
            "members discover and buy right here. no redirecting
            to a checkout page on another domain."

1:40-1:55   THE XP MOMENT (the differentiator)
            Show XP gain, level-up animation.
            "and this is what keeps them coming back. every action
            earns XP. your community has progression built in."

1:55-2:10   FACE ON (cam-only, back from screen share)
            "that's the whole thing. one place instead of discord
            plus teachable plus notion plus a community platform.
            your members walk through your world instead of
            toggling between tabs."

2:10-2:20   [PAUSE - personalized close gets spliced here for
            top-10, or generic close plays for volume tier]
```

**Total narration: ~280 words.** ~30 seconds of visual silence where the
product speaks for itself.

### Generic close (volume tier - plays after demo)

```
2:10-2:20   FACE ON
            "if this looks like something your community could
            use, reply to this email and i'll send a calendly."
```

### Pacing rules

- 140-150 words per minute (conversational, not presenter pace)
- 1-breath pause between sections (silence lets things land)
- Never narrate what they can already see on screen
- When the visual is impressive, shut up for 3-5 seconds

## Type 2: Personalized Intro (30 sec, top-10 only)

**Total target: 30 seconds spoken = ~75-90 words.**

This gets spliced BEFORE the reusable demo. Three beats + a close after
the demo.

### Beat structure

```
0:00-0:03   FACE ON (cam-only)
            Their website or content VISIBLE ON YOUR SCREEN behind
            you - or pull it up immediately.
            "[name], wanted to send you something quick."
            [Say their name in the first 2 seconds. This is the
            #1 predictor of whether they keep watching.]

0:03-0:12   SCREEN SHARE showing THEIR content
            Pull up their Skool page, tweet, YouTube video, or
            community. Show it on screen while talking.
            "[THE SPECIFIC NOTICE - reference the exact thing.
            Quote their words if possible.]"
            Example: "saw your post last week about discord
            notifications - the part about 'i'd unsubscribe from
            my own server if i could' got me."
            [Showing their content on screen is the single
            highest-impact personalization signal for video.]

0:12-0:22   FACE ON (back to cam)
            "[THE BRIDGE - one sentence connecting their thing
            to what they're about to see.]"
            Example: "we built basically what we wished existed
            when that stopped working for us."

0:22-0:27   SETUP
            "let me show you."
            [Then the recording cuts to the pre-recorded demo.]

--- DEMO PLAYS (2:00) ---

AFTER DEMO - PERSONALIZED CLOSE (10 sec)

            FACE ON
            "[name], if [community_name] feels anything like
            that - [one specific mapping: their tavern with the
            N members, their academy holding the cohort content]
            - reply here and i'll send a calendly."
```

### Template (fill from `memory/people/<slug>.md`)

```
[NAME], wanted to send something quick.

[ONE_SPECIFIC_NOTICE - recent video, milestone, post, complaint.
 Quote their words if possible. Show their content on screen.]

[BRIDGE - one sentence. If they complained about discord: "this is
 basically what we built when that stopped working for us." If they
 hit a milestone: "your members are at the size where this starts
 mattering." If they expressed an interest: "this is what that looks
 like when you build it for real."]

let me show you.

[CUT TO REUSABLE 2-MIN DEMO]

[NAME], if [COMMUNITY_NAME] feels anything like that - your
[SPECIFIC_THING - tavern / academy / XP system mapping] - reply
on [channel] and i'll send a calendly.
```

### Worked example

**Lead:** Sarah Kim, runs "Founders Forge" Skool community (1,400
members), recently posted complaining about Discord notification chaos.

```
sarah, wanted to send something quick.

[SCREEN: show her Skool post about Discord notifications]
saw your post last week about discord notifications eating into your
deep-work time - the part about "i'd unsubscribe from my own server
if i could" got me.

[FACE: back to cam]
we built basically what we wished existed when that stopped working
for us. let me show you.

[2-MIN DEMO]

[FACE: cam for close]
sarah, if founders forge feels anything like that - your tavern with
the 1,400 of them actually together, your academy holding the cohort
content, no notifications screaming - reply here and i'll send a
calendly.
```

## Recording protocol (for Ibi)

### Pre-recording checklist

- [ ] Close all tabs except the product and their website/content
- [ ] Clean desktop, no notifications visible, no bookmarks bar
- [ ] Loom set to Screen+Cam mode (face bubble bottom-left)
- [ ] Their website/Skool/content loaded in a browser tab ready to show
- [ ] 3 bullet points written down (not a script): notice, bridge, setup
- [ ] Good lighting on face, camera at eye level
- [ ] Product loaded with real-looking demo data (not "Test User")

### Recording order

1. Record personalized intro (30 sec) - one take
2. Splice in pre-recorded demo (already saved)
3. Record personalized close (10 sec) - one take
4. Set Loom CTA button to "Reply to chat"
5. Title: `[first name] - [community name] in arcadia`

### Quality gates - do NOT re-record for

- Minor verbal stumbles (authenticity signal)
- Slightly different wording than the bullet points (means you're
  talking naturally, not reading)
- A brief pause to collect thoughts (human, not robotic)

### DO re-record if

- You said the wrong name
- You referenced the wrong community/content
- Technical glitch (frozen screen, audio cut)
- Over 40 seconds on the intro

Total recording time per lead: 3-5 min with demo prepared.

---

## Demo recording session checklist (one-time)

- [ ] Product loaded with demo data that looks real
- [ ] Avatar styled to look professional but approachable
- [ ] All 4 locations pre-loaded and tested (World, Tavern, Academy, Market)
- [ ] XP/level-up moment set up to trigger on cue
- [ ] Practice the walk path 2-3 times before recording
- [ ] Total narration: ~280 words for 2 minutes (rest is visual silence)
- [ ] Record 3 takes, pick the one that sounds most natural (not most polished)

---

## What to NEVER do in the Loom intro

- Read from a script word-for-word (memorize the 3 beats, then talk)
- Start with "Hi [name], my name is [yours] and I'm the founder of..."
- Pitch features in the intro (the demo carries features)
- Mention price (save for the Calendly call)
- Try to be funny if it's not natural
- Record a perfectly polished take (minor imperfections = authenticity)
- Send a screen-only recording (face on at open and close = 3x reply rate)
- Use a thumbnail that shows a blank screen or loading page
